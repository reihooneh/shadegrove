Working out the plan…

How a plan is made

  1. Trace the sun

    Every quarter of an hour, the sun's position for that place and date, and which bits of footpath buildings and existing trees already shade.

  2. Weigh the hours

    Sun at 3 pm on a 38 °C day matters far more than sun at 9 am, and a busy footpath more than a quiet one.

  3. Place trees one by one

    Each step adds the tree that removes the most of the sun still left, after the trees already chosen, at its size in ten years.

  4. Prove how close it is

    Because each new tree helps less than the last, the planner can put a hard ceiling on what any plan could achieve, and show how near its own plan comes.
